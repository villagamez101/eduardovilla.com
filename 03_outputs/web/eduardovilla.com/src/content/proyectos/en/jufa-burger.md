      Jufa Burger is a burger joint in Valle de Chalco, State of Mexico. The

      accessible price, for a mostly young audience (70%) with an adult core
      (30%). The brief wasn't to invent a new restaurant but to give the brand
      an image it would be recognized by at a glance: on the street sign, on
      the packaging and at the table.

      layers and a single silhouette, readable at any size, from the sign down
      to the favicon. The construction came from adding three ideas together:
      the hamburger itself, the delight of food and the quality of the
      ingredient.
